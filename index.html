Two specialist firms, one contractor

Technology World brings together a rotating-equipment specialist and a power-and-oil construction contractor, each run by its founder for well over a decade.

  • 2006Omar Emad El Din co-founds UniTech and manages all its projects until 2016.
  • 2009Saad Abdul-Jabbar founds Technology World, specialising in alignment, installation and maintenance of pumps, compressors and turbines.
  • 2017Omar Emad El Din founds Wide Gate Tech for power and oil construction, supply and E/M/I testing.
  • TodayBoth companies operate as one: Technology World Co.

Vision

To be a leading provider of innovative and sustainable solutions in the power and oil sectors, contributing to the growth of the industries we serve.

Mission

To deliver high-quality services that exceed our clients' expectations, with safety, reliability and efficiency in every operation and long-term relationships built on trust and integrity.

From first drawing to first start-up
